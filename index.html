Roof, Eavestrough & Garage Extension

The old roof was the barn's most pressing problem — a liability that made everything underneath uncertain. New roofing, proper eavestroughing, and the garage extension change the fundamental character of the building from the moment you arrive.

What it could be

A protected, weather-tight structure that sets the tone for everything inside — a barn that looks cared for from the road and earns trust before anyone steps through the door.

Six New Windows & New Roof Line

The second set of six windows along the right side of the barn transforms what was a dark interior into a space flooded with natural light. Paired with the new roof and eavestrough, the whole right face of the barn reads as intentional rather than incidental.

What it could be

Light is the one thing you can't retrofit into a space cheaply. This barn already has it — six windows of golden afternoon light that make any interior use, from events to studios to rentals, instantly more valuable.

03
Tractor Shed → Three Covered Bays

Barely holding together, the old tractor shed was one hard winter away from becoming a problem. What's taken its place is three distinct covered bays — one for the tractor, one for the car, one for tools — each properly sheltered from snow and rain under a new roof.

What it could be

A fully functional outbuilding that pays for itself in protected storage alone — and makes the whole property feel organised, deliberate, and ready for whatever comes next.
